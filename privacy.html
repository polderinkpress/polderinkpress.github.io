Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Polder Ink Press publishes the Cozy States coloring books. This page explains what information this website and our own tools handle. Questions? Email polderinkpress@gmail.com.

This website

This website has no accounts, contact forms, analytics or advertising cookies, and we do not collect personal information through it. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; like any web server, these services may process your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages. Their own privacy policies apply. Links to our books lead to Amazon, which has its own privacy policy.

Free printable coloring pages

The free coloring pages can be downloaded directly. We do not ask for your name or email address.

Amazon Ads reporting tool

We use a small private tool, connected through Login with Amazon, to download performance reports for our own Amazon Ads campaigns (for example impressions, clicks, spend and search terms). The tool only accesses our own advertising account, with our permission. The reports are stored privately on our own computer, are used only to improve our own advertising, and are never sold or shared with third parties. We do not access other advertisers' data or any customer personal information.

Social media

We share our coloring pages on Pinterest and Instagram. When you interact with us there, the privacy policies of those platforms apply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
